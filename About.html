Our Story

Powering Kampala Since 2015

Mugagga Electronics was founded in 2015 by Mugagga Agnes with a clear vision: to provide Kampala's homes, businesses, and construction projects with access to genuine, high-quality electrical products at fair, competitive prices. What began as a small shop on Kampala Road has grown into one of the city's most trusted electrical supplies businesses.

Over the years, we have expanded our product range significantly — from basic switches and cables to complete solar energy systems, circuit protection equipment, and electrical appliances. We have also added professional installation and consultancy services to serve the full needs of our customers.

Today, Mugagga Electronics serves thousands of customers across Kampala and Uganda, including homeowners, electricians, contractors, businesses, schools, and government institutions. Our commitment to quality, genuine products, and excellent customer service remains unchanged from day one.

CEO Profile

Mugagga Agnes

Founder & Chief Executive Officer
"My commitment has always been to provide Ugandans with access to genuine, high-quality electrical products at prices they can afford. Safety in electrical installations starts with the quality of products used — and that is something I refuse to compromise on."

Mugagga Agnes is the visionary founder of Mugagga Electronics. Since establishing the business in 2015, she has led with a hands-on approach — building supplier relationships, training staff, and personally ensuring every product on the shelves meets her exacting standards.

Her philosophy is rooted in service: she believes that a successful business is one that genuinely improves the lives of its customers. Under her leadership, Mugagga Electronics has grown from a small shop into a full-service electrical solutions provider trusted by thousands across Uganda.
